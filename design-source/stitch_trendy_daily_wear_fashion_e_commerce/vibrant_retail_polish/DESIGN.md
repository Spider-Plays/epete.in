## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-energy, editorial fast-fashion retail experience tailored for digitally fluent, trend-conscious shoppers. It marries European minimalism with high-velocity e-commerce mechanics: hyper-legible typographic rhythms, clean white backdrops, tactile card groupings, and urgent, saturated accents.

### Design Principles
- **Editorial Polish Meets Velocity:** Balance magazine-grade product discovery with conversion-centric friction reduction. Clean margins and airy negative space keep dense multi-attribute catalogs feeling curated rather than cluttered.
- **Affirmative Feedback & Visual Momentum:** Interactive triggers—quick-size selectors, dynamic wishlist toggles, and dual-action checkout anchors—respond instantly with subtle tactile micro-elevations and chromatic feedback.
- **Scannable Information Hierarchy:** Product metadata adheres to an uncompromising, repeatable visual hierarchy: bold price anchoring, distinct strikethrough references, micro-metered review summaries, and promotional pills that capture peripheral focus without degrading baseline scannability.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical clarity with dynamic conversion signals. High-reflectance neutral grounds (#FFFFFF, #F8F9FA, #F1F3F5) ensure merchandise photography remains the absolute hero, while razor-sharp functional tints guide user eye flow through the checkout funnel.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#E11D48`):** The catalytic core for immediate conversion actions, primary buttons, wishlist toggles, key micro-badges, and sale notifications.
- **Secondary Accent (`#4F46E5`):** Reserved for loyalty systems, exclusive drops, category filter highlights, and contextual guidance chips.
- **Success & Value (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Applied strictly to price savings, promotional calculations (e.g., "40% OFF"), in-stock indicators, and validated checkout states.
- **Urgency & Social Proof (`#F59E0B`):** Dedicated to star ratings, customer review aggregations, flash deal countdowns, and low-inventory warnings.
- **Neutral & Surface Foundations:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FFFFFF`
  - Secondary Canvas & Well: `#F8F9FA`
  - Neutral Soft Surface: `#F1F3F5`
  - Hairline Dividers & Outlines: `#E2E8F0` and `#E9ECEF`
  - High-Contrast Primary Text: `#111827`
  - Mid-Contrast Subtext & Labels: `#4B5563`
  - Muted Placeholders & Inactive States: `#9CA3AF`

## Typography

The typography couples the sculptural, contemporary geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the rhythmic readability and neutral density of **Hanken Grotesk**.

### Typographic Roles
- **Headlines & Editorial Promos (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Tightly tracked display and headline styles create brand confidence and editorial punch. Use bold and extra-bold weights for campaign anchors and product category markers.
- **Body & Product Descriptors (Hanken Grotesk):** Provides frictionless readability across multi-line garment descriptions, size guides, reviews, and specification tables.
- **Pricing & Functional Numeric Data:** Discounted transaction prices deploy `price-headline` using Plus Jakarta Sans for instant visual weight; strikethrough MSRP values adopt muted neutral styling in Hanken Grotesk.
- **Micro-Badges & Interactive Tags:** Render all badges, category pills, and discount indicators using uppercase or tracking-boosted `label-sm` or `label-md` to ensure immediate legibility at compact scales.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly regimented 8pt spatial baseline maintains rhythm across browsing grids and dense transactional views.

### Grid Systems & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Product listings render either as full-width hero cards or a balanced 2-column continuous scroll.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin-tablet: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Product grids adapt to a 3-column configuration; faceted filters tuck into an off-canvas drawer.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with `margin-desktop: 2.5rem` (max-width capped at 1440px) and `gutter-desktop: 1.5rem`. A sticky 280px left sidebar anchors faceted filtering, accompanied by an adaptive 4-column product card stream.

### Spacing Usage
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for internal component micro-spacing (e.g., space between price blocks, star icons, and inline badges).
- Use `space-md` for standard card content padding, field gaps, and input margins.
- Use `space-xl` through `space-3xl` for section separation, editorial blocks, and banner margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages sharp hairline containment combined with warm, tinted ambient elevation. Flat, stark drops are replaced by diffuse color-aware layering that softens the retail interface while guiding attention.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base page background (`#FFFFFF`) and section backdrops (`#F8F9FA`). No shadow; separation is achieved via `1px` borders using `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Standard product cards, filter blocks, and coupon containers.
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`
  - Border: `1px solid #F1F3F5`
- **Level 2 (Hover State & Flyouts):** Active cards on pointer hover, interactive size selector overlays, dropdowns, and mega-menu flyouts.
  - Shadow: `0 10px 20px -5px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`
  - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers & Modals):** Sticky search and navigation headers, bottom sheets on mobile, and quick-view modals.
  - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`
  - Surface: Translucent `#FFFFFF` at 95% opacity with `12px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **Level 4 (Transactional Floating Anchors):** Floating sticky "Add to Bag" bars on mobile PDPs.
  - Shadow: `0 -4px 16px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08)`

## Shapes

The system relies on roundedness level `2` (0.5rem / 8px base radius), producing a modern, friendly silhouette that retains clean alignment across high-density listings.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Interactive Foundations (`rounded` = 0.5rem / 8px):** Standard buttons, text inputs, dropdown triggers, and faceted filter check-containers.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-lg` = 1rem / 16px):** Product cards, promo banners, modal dialogs, and coupon callout blocks.
- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full` = 9999px):** Category tag chips, discount micro-badges, quick-size circle buttons, notification counters, and floating wishlist icons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ('Buy Now'):** Solid `#E11D48` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 700`, `rounded: 0.5rem`. Height: 48px desktop / 44px mobile. Hover triggers `#BE123C` with Level 2 elevation.
- **Secondary CTA ('Add to Bag'):** Crisp `#111827` solid background with `#FFFFFF` text, or high-contrast border variant with 1.5px outline in `#111827`, `#111827` text, and subtle `#F8F9FA` hover fill.
- **Quick-Add Micro Buttons:** Circular or pill-shaped buttons with smooth scaling transforms (`scale(1.04)`) upon hover.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean 1:1 or 4:5 image container with overflow hidden, capped by a 16px card radius.
- **Badge Anchors:** Absolute top-left container housing stacked pill micro-badges (e.g., "BESTSELLER" in `#111827` dark fill, "40% OFF" in `#059669` green fill).
- **Wishlist Toggle:** Floating 36px circle on top-right, Level 1 shadow, `#FFFFFF` translucent ground, housing a vector heart that transitions to solid `#E11D48` upon selection.
- **Quick-Size Selector:** Hover-revealed bottom slide-over strip on desktop; direct touch scroll on mobile. Shows standard size pills (XS, S, M, L, XL) with immediate visual disablement for out-of-stock sizes.
- **Metadata Block:** Brand title in `label-sm` muted uppercase; product name in `body-md` single-line truncated text; price block showing discounted bold price (`price-headline`), strikethrough MRP (`price-strikethrough`), and discount percentage in bold `#059669`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Selection Pills:** Height 32px, `rounded-full`, 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0`, `#4B5563` text.
- **Active State:** Solid `#111827` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, or tinted `#E11D48` background at 10% opacity with `#E11D48` border and label text.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** 44px height, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, `#F8F9FA` background transitioning to `#FFFFFF` on focus, bounded by a 2px `#E11D48` focus ring with zero outline displacement.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px square/circle with 1.5px border `#9CA3AF`. Checked state transitions instantly to `#E11D48` fill with crisp white internal glyph.

### Navigation & Header
- **Sticky Header:** 72px desktop / 56px mobile height, Level 3 elevated frosted glass. Integrated high-speed search bar with inline category chips, real-time suggestion dropdowns, and counter badges over cart/wishlist icons using `#E11D48` fill and `#FFFFFF` micro-text.

### Checkout & Conversion Modules
- **Coupon Cards:** Dashed 1.5px border in `#059669` with tinted emerald surface (`#ECFDF5`), bold coupon code token, and one-click "APPLY" action.
- **Progress Trackers:** Linear multi-step indicator utilizing `#059669` for validated stages and `#E11D48` for the active step.
